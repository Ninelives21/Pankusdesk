1.0

PREAMBLE:

1.1 VNR Vignana Jyothi Institute of Engineering and Technology (VNRVJIET) is committed to achieving academic excellence through innovative teaching-learning processes, continuous curriculum enhancement and robust evaluation mechanisms.

1.2 In line with the guidelines of the University Grants Commission (UGC), All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad (JNTUH), the Institute introduced the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) for all Undergraduate and Postgraduate Programmes from the academic year 2015-

2016 and continues its implementation under the Academic Regulations stipulated herein.

1.3 The implementation of the CBCS at VNRVJIET is intended to align with national initiatives for standardization of higher education and to enhance student flexibility, academic mobility, interdisciplinary learning and skill development.

2.0 SHORT TITLE, APPLICATION AND COMMENCEMENT:

2.1 These Regulations shall be called the “VNRVJIET B.Tech. Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) Academic Regulations, 2025” and may be referred to as the “VNRVJIET B.Tech. CBCS R25 Regulations”.

2.2 These Regulations shall apply to all B.Tech. Regular (Full-Time) Undergraduate Programmes offered by the Institute.

2.3 These Regulations shall come into force for all student batches admitted from the academic year 2025-2026 onwards.

2.4 These Regulations are comprehensive and include, inter alia, provisions relating to the definitions of key terms, the semester system, the credit system, the grading system, Programme Structure, the Scheme of Examinations and Evaluation procedures.

2.5 The Institute may, from time to time, revise, amend, or update these Regulations, the scheme of examinations and the syllabi, based on the recommendations of the respective Boards of Studies (BoS), subject to the approval of the Academic Council.

2.6 Any such revisions, amendments, or updates shall take effect from the date specified and shall be applicable to such batches of students, including those already undergoing the programme, as approved by the Academic Council.

3.0

CHOICE BASED CREDIT SYSTEM:

3.1 The Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) is an academic framework that enables a student to qualify for the award of a degree by earning the prescribed number of credits as specified in the Programme Curriculum. The credits earned represent the academic workload and learning effort associated with a course.

3.2 Each course is assigned a fixed number of credits based on its depth, breadth and intended learning outcomes. Student performance in each course is evaluated through a grading system and the grade points obtained reflect the level of proficiency achieved.

3.3 The CBCS offers the following features and advantages:

i. Flexibility in course selection: Students may choose elective courses from a broad spectrum offered by various departments. ii. Opportunities for additional learning: Students may register for courses beyond the minimum prescribed requirements to enhance their knowledge and skills. iii. Interdisciplinary learning: Students may adopt an interdisciplinary approach by selecting courses across different domains. iv. Academic mobility: Credits earned from other departments or recognized higher education institutions in India may be transferred, subject to approval as per Institute norms.

7.0

ELIGIBILITY FOR ADMISSION:

7.1 The eligibility criteria for admission to the B.Tech. Degree Programmes offered at VNRVJIET shall be as prescribed by the Government of Telangana and in accordance with the statutory provisions in force.

7.2 Unless otherwise notified by the Government of Telangana, the criteria shall be as follows:

a) The candidate shall be an Indian National or a Non-Resident Indian (NRI).

b) The candidate shall have completed sixteen (16) years of age as on 31 December of the Academic Year in which admission is sought.

c) The candidate shall have passed the qualifying examination (10+2 or equivalent) as on the date of admission, as recognized by the Board of Intermediate Education (BIE), Telangana State.

7.3 The seats in each programme of the Institute shall be classified into the following categories, in accordance with the Government Orders in force:

i) Category-A Seats: These shall be filled through the centralized admission counselling process, based on the rank secured by the candidate in the Engineering, Agriculture and Pharmacy Common Entrance Test (EAPCET) conducted by the Government of Telangana.

ii) Category-B Seats: These shall be filled by the Institute in accordance with the Government Orders issued by the Government of Telangana from time to time.

7.4 Direct Admission to Second Year (Lateral Entry Scheme): A candidate shall be eligible for admission directly into the III Semester (II Year I Semester) of the B.Tech. Degree Programme under the Lateral Entry Scheme, based on the rank secured in the Engineering Common Entrance Test [ECET (for Diploma Holders)] conducted by the Government of Telangana and subject to compliance with the other admission criteria prescribed in the Government Orders in force.

9.0 B.TECH. PROGRAMME STRUCTURE:

9.1 The structure of the B.Tech. Degree Programmes offered at VNRVJIET shall be in accordance with the Choice Based Credit System (CBCS) and the Credit Based Semester System (CBSS), as defined by the University Grants Commission (UGC) and aligned with the Model Curriculum prescribed by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE) and Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad (JNTUH).

9.2 The total credits prescribed for B.Tech. Degree Programme shall be 164, out of which a student shall earn a minimum of 160 credits for the award of the B.Tech. Degree.

9.3 Semester Scheme:

9.3.1 The B.Tech. Degree Programmes shall follow the semester scheme with the following provisions:

9.3.1.1 Each academic year shall consist of two (2) semesters, namely Odd and Even semesters, sequentially numbered from I to VIII Semester.

9.3.1.3 Each semester shall comprise Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE) and Semester End Examination (SEE) components, as prescribed in these Regulations.

10.0 DURATION OF THE ACADEMIC PROGRAMMES:

10.1 The normal duration of the B.Tech. Degree Programme shall be as follows: i. Four (4) academic years for students admitted to the I Year of the programme.

ii. Three (3) academic years for students admitted under the Lateral Entry Scheme (LES), i.e., Diploma Holders admitted directly into the III Semester (II Year I Semester).

10.2 The maximum permissible duration for completion of the programme shall be twice the normal duration, as given below: i. Eight (8) academic years for students admitted to the I Year.

ii. Six (6) academic years for students admitted under the Lateral Entry Scheme (LES).

23.0 COMPUTATION OF SGPA AND CGPA:

23.1 Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA):

23.1.1 The SGPA shall be computed only when a student has cleared all the courses registered in that semester.

23.1.2 The SGPA shall be calculated using the formula:

\[\mathrm{SGPA}=\dfrac{\text{Total earned weighted grade points in a semester}}{\text{Total credits in a semester}}\]
\[\mathrm{SGPA}=\dfrac{\sum_{i=1}^{P}C_i G_i}{\sum_{i=1}^{P}C_i}\]

Where: Ci = Number of credits allotted to a particular course ‘i’ Gi = Grade point corresponding to the letter grade awarded to the course ‘i’ i = 1,2,…..p represent the number of courses in a particular semester

23.1.3 The SGPA shall be rounded off to two decimal places.

23.1.4 The SGPA of a semester shall be printed on the Memorandum of Grades only in accordance with the provisions of Clause 22.7 of these Regulations.

23.2 Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA):

23.2.1 The CGPA shall be computed after the completion of each semester from II semester onwards.

23.2.2 The CGPA shall be calculated using the formula:

\[\mathrm{CGPA}=\dfrac{\text{Total earned weighted grade points for the entire programme}}{\text{Total credits for the entire programme}}\]

PDF page 36

\[\mathrm{CGPA}=\dfrac{\sum_{j=1}^{m}C_j G_j}{\sum_{j=1}^{m}C_j}\]

Where: Cj = number of credits allotted to a particular course ‘j’ Gj = grade point corresponding to the letter grade awarded to that course ‘j’ j = 1,2,….m represent the number of courses of the entire programme

23.2.3 Only the best 160 credits earned by the student out of all registered courses shall be considered for CGPA computation.

23.2.4 The CGPA shall be rounded off to two decimal places.

23.2.5 Grade lower than ‘C’ shall not be considered for CGPA computation.

23.2.6 The CGPA shall be awarded only after the student earns the required 160 credits for the program.

c) The student has completed all additional requirements as specified under Clause

27.3 of the Regulations.
