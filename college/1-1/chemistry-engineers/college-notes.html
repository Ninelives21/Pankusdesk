Printing and scope

Start with the R25 unit notes linked above and use your browser’s Print command. Source inventories and older-syllabus extras are not additional compulsory reading. Select topics before printing a lengthy reference collection.

Supporting sources contribute only the selections explicitly cited. “Held” means inventoried, not fully transcribed or academically verified. Raw PDFs, PPTs and private photographs are not published. No new technical drawings are produced in this consolidation.

Authoring and source qualifications remain visible on the notes pages. These are supplied senior/college materials; lecturer approval is not inferred. Textbook Notes remain a separate collection on the Chemistry index.
