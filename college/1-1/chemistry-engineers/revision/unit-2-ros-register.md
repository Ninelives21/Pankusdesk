## Source limitations

- Both supplied SHE drawings contain an erroneous Hg/Mercury label. ROS retains the pixels; every placement carries the explicit correction that SHE uses platinized platinum.
- The sacrificial composite’s left-panel caption is misleading: Mg is consumed and the base metal is protected. This is explained at every placement.
- Small waterline/galvanic/Calomel figures remain low-resolution. Full-size opening is available; no upscaling or sharpening was applied.
- The impressed-current source is faint; linework is retained unchanged.
- The original illustrated summary is already clipped at the bottom. Its complete applications are provided in the adjacent accessible HTML table; no pixels were reconstructed.
- The source oxidation panel uses schematic MO for variable valency. A correction caption and balanced equation accompany it.
