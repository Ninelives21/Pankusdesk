## Consistency correction

In the second photographed example, the `(3,2)` entry appears without a minus sign. With `+1`, the displayed matrix has eigenvalues `2,4,5`, contradicting the exercise statement that `3` and `6` are eigenvalues. With `(3,2)=-1`, the matrix has eigenvalues `2,3,6`, exactly matching the class working. The student-facing dated notes therefore use the corrected intended sign; this provenance note records the correction.
