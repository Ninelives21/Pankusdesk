## Assessment Q2 — Thevenin’s theorem

Find the current flowing through the 2-ohm resistor connected between the points \(a\) and \(b\) using Thevenin’s theorem.

### Verified direction check

For the 12 V source polarity drawn in the question (left rail positive, right rail negative), the open-circuit node voltages are

\[
V_a=7.2\ \text{V},\qquad V_b=7.5\ \text{V}.
\]

Hence

\[
V_{ab}=V_a-V_b=-0.3\ \text{V}.
\]

Therefore point \(b\) is 0.3 V above point \(a\). With the 2-ohm resistor reconnected, the current magnitude is

\[
I=47.8\ \text{mA},
\]

flowing from \(b\) to \(a\). If current is referenced from \(a\) to \(b\), then \(I_{ab}=-47.8\ \text{mA}\).
