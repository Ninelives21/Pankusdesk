Introduction to electrical engineering and D C circuits

### Circuit concept

- Electric circuit consists of closed path interconnecting active and passive elements
- Components are named as circuit elements
- The closed path followed by direct current (DC) is called DC circuit
- The closed path followed by AC current is called AC circuit.
- The source supplies electrical energy to the load which converts it into heat or other forms of energy. In a DC-circuit, loads may be connected in series, parallel, or series-parallel.

### Definition of various terms

**Charge:** Charge is the characteristic property of an elementric particle of the matter, such as protons, neutrons, and electrons.

## Page 2

- Protons possess + charge, electrons possess - charge and neutrons possess neutral.
- The unit of charge is coulomb (C)

### Current

A steady current can be represented as \(I=\dfrac{Q}{T}\) where \(Q=\text{charge}\), \(T=\text{time}\).

A time varying current can be expressed as \(i=\dfrac{dq}{dt}\)

- It is defined as rate of change of charge passing through the cross sectional area of the conductor

Unit of current is amperes (A)

### Voltage/absolute potential

- It is defined a amount of work required to move a point from infinity to a point in the field
- Unit : Volts (V)

### Potential difference

- The amount of work to be done to move a unit positive test charge from one place to another in the field.

\[V=\dfrac{W}{Q}\qquad \text{Steady voltage}\]

## Page 3

- Time-varying voltage:

\[dV=\dfrac{dW}{dQ}\]

Unit of potential difference : (Volts)

### Energy

Capacity to do work. The energy may exist in many forms such as electrical, mechanical, thermal, etc.

- Unit: Joules (J)

### Power

Rate at which electrical energy is converted to other forms of energy [crossed out / illegible] \(=\) product of voltage and current.

\[P=\dfrac{dW}{dt}=VI\]
